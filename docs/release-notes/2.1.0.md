Awake 2.1.0, the first release on Google Play.

- Keep your laptop awake from the taskbar icon, its window, a Quick Settings tile or a desktop widget: until you turn
  it off, or for 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours.
- Battery and lid guards turn Awake off when the battery runs low or the lid closes.
- The accessibility-service fallback (a keep-screen-on overlay and simulated taps) is gone: Awake keeps the device
  awake with a foreground service and a wake lock alone, and needs no accessibility permission.
- Signed with a new release key, the same one Google Play uses. If you installed 2.0.0 from GitHub, uninstall it
  before installing this version.

Awake has no internet permission, no ads and no accounts, and collects nothing.
