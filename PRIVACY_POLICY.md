# Awake privacy policy

The published policy is at **https://meridian11.com/privacy/awake**; this copy says the same.

Last updated 3 November 2026. For the Awake app for Android (`com.meridian11.books.awake`), made and published on
Google Play by Meridian11 LLC.

## The short version

Awake keeps your device from going to sleep while you want it awake. It has no internet permission, no accounts, no
ads, analytics or trackers, and it collects and shares nothing. Its settings stay on your device, and Meridian11
receives nothing from it.

## Permissions and what they are for

| Permission | What Awake uses it for |
|---|---|
| Keep the device awake | Holding the screen and processor awake while you have Awake turned on, and letting go when you turn it off or its timer ends |
| Foreground service | Staying active while it keeps the device awake, with a notification that shows it is on and turns it off in one tap |
| Notifications (optional) | That notification, with its Turn off and +30 minutes buttons |
| Run at startup | Resetting the app icon and the switch to off after the device restarts or Awake is updated |

Awake can turn itself off when the battery runs low or the lid closes, and on when you connect the charger; you
choose which of these it does in its settings. It learns of them from Android's own signals, which need no permission
and are never stored or sent.

## What Awake keeps

Your settings (whether Awake is on, the timer you chose, and its options) stay in Awake's private app storage on your
device. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include them in your device backup, in your own Google
account. That backup is managed by Android and your account; Meridian11 can't see it. Uninstalling Awake deletes its
data from the device.

## Google Play

If you install Awake from Google Play, Google Play gives Meridian11 statistics such as install counts, and reports of
crashes and freezes from people who chose to share them with app developers. These come from Google Play, not from
Awake; they don't identify you, and Google's privacy policy covers them.

## Children

Awake isn't directed at children under 13, and Meridian11 receives nothing from anyone who uses it.
