Awake keeps your laptop from going to sleep while you want it awake: during a long download, a presentation, a video call or a build, or while you read. Click the coffee cup in the taskbar and the screen stays on. Click it again and your laptop sleeps as usual.

One switch, four places
Turn Awake on and off from its icon in the taskbar, from its window, from a Quick Settings tile, or from a widget on your desktop. All four always show the same state, and the taskbar icon itself changes: a full cup while Awake is on, a half-empty one when it is off.

For as long as you choose
Keep awake until you turn it off, or for 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours. While Awake is on, a notification shows it, with buttons to turn it off or add 30 minutes.

Guards you can switch on and off
• Battery guard: turns Awake off when the battery drops below 20% while unplugged (on by default).
• Lid guard: turns Awake off when you close the lid, so your laptop sleeps in your bag (on by default).
• Charger: turns Awake on when you plug in the charger (off by default).
• Screen dimming: lets the screen dim while the laptop stays awake.

Quick to reach
Right-click the taskbar icon for its shortcuts. Or set the icon to switch Awake on and off at once, without opening a window. The widget starts as a single square and shows the timer and settings when you make it bigger.

Private
Awake has no internet permission, no ads and no accounts, and collects nothing. Its settings stay on your device.
